Reading the result:
  Lists coordinators with observed workflow runs for this repository in
  the last 30 days, across commits and branches, plus coordinators that
  were requested, claim to serve it, or advertise readiness for it without
  recent runs. Readiness is a discovery hint for choosing a coordinator to
  request, not a promise of acceptance. Maintainer requests, the
  coordinator's own service claim, its advertised availability and the
  authorization of individual runs are separate facts: a run does not prove
  a standing service agreement, and a claim does not prove that a workflow
  is eligible or that the coordinator is connected.

  This is observed history, not an archive (JSON `history_complete: false`):
  relays may prune evidence, so absence never proves that service stopped.
  Coordinator labels are verified NIP-05 identities, never profile display
  names, with the npub always shown; JSON has `verified_identity`, the full
  `request_history`, activity counts, latest-run evidence and relay
  failures.
